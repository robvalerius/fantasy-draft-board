Two buckets that matter, plus a drawer for everyone else:
  • 🔥 Must-watch / ☠️ Big threat — in more than one starting lineup
  • ✅ In your lineup / 👎 Starting against you — in exactly one lineup
  • 👀 Bench / 💤 Their bench — single flame or thumb, nobody is starting him, so his points don't count anywhere. Tucked into a collapsed drawer at the bottom of each column and dimmed. Tap the header to open it.
Inside a bucket, the flame and thumb count just sorts by league size — 4 = SHERIFF, 3 = VALERIAN, 2 = CHAMP or Guest of Honor, 1 = bench. League is never its own bucket; use the league chips up top if you want to filter to one.

Bubbles: a green bubble is a league where he's on your roster, a red bubble is a league where he's on the team you're playing. Faded bubbles are bench spots. A player with both is flagged ⚔ and sits on whichever side matters more.

Live stats: tap 📡 Live stats to pull real NFL box-score numbers from Sleeper's public feed every 60 seconds — passing and rushing and receiving yards, catches, TDs, kicking points and misses. Only numbers above zero ever appear, so a quiet row means a quiet day. These are raw stats, not fantasy points, so they are the same no matter which league you are looking at.

All kickoff times are Pacific. Use the When row to zero in on a single window — tap one to see just that slate, tap more to add them.
